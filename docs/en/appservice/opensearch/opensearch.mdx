Alauda Data Services Search Engine E1 is a search and analytics engine service built on the open-source OpenSearch, for full-text search, log analytics and observability workloads. It deploys and manages OpenSearch clusters and OpenSearch Dashboards on Kubernetes, with automated provisioning, scaling, upgrades and full lifecycle management.
